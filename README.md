# Sales & Profitability

## Overview

This was an analytical project, focused on analysing sales and profitability within the Tailwind Traders dataset, Data manipulation, calculations, currency conversion and interactive reports in Power BI were performed.

## Tools Used

### 1.Microsoft Excel :  
Data manipulation, calculation, Financial Analysis

### 2.Power Query :  
Data manipulation and data profiling

### 3.Python :  
Currency conversion

### 4.Power BI :  
Data Modelling, DAX, Time Intelligence and Visualisations

### 5.Power BI Service :   
Publishing dashboards and Subscriptions

## Process  
- Sales data was prepared and revenue, costs and profits calculated in Excel.  
- Data was manipulated in Power Query.  
- Currency conversion data was integrated using Python.  
- A date dimension was created and DAX measures were built.  
- Report 1 (Sales Overview) and Report 2 (Profit Overview) were created.  
- Reports were published to Power BI Service. An Executive Dashboard was created.

## Key Insights
Note 1: An artificial data set was used for this analysis. Values including the constant profit margin of 62.37% are an artefact of the data that was produced. The intention was to showcase the analysis and dashboards process.

### Product and inventory performance
Overall, the organisation had net sales of ~$4.06K. Sales were predominantly from a select range of products. Bamboo Plant Pot topped sales with 710 (approximately 17%), then Brass Coat Rack (399) and Velvet Cushion Cover (375). Products towards the bottom of sales were selling for around 130. Sales by volume show that best-selling products are largely for the garden/outdoors; there are 4 of these (Cordless Leaf Blower, Garden Glove Set, LED Garden Lights and Solar Outdoor Lantern). However, the majority of products on the catalogue only sold 1 or 2. This indicates that there is a long tail of under-performing products. Further, only 152 items were bought despite there being 13,635 in stock – a 90:1 ratio. This indicates significant overstock and therefore that funds are bound up in stock.

### Regional customer behaviour
Looking at different regions, the UAE is both a high value and disengaged region. It has the highest median sales (~681 which is 45% of the overall cross-regional median sales figure) yet the lowest level of loyalty points (151). The UK (315) and USA (305) have the highest levels of customer loyalty, then Australia (262) and France (197). Although UAE consumers spend more per sale, they are not being successfully re-engaged. Therefore, the UAE should be targeted for loyalty and re-engagement schemes. Conversely, the UK and USA should be targeted for further upselling. Median sales were also very changeable. 

### Sales Trend
There was a large peak around 998 in early September 2023, followed by a sharp decline. There was then a partial rebound in October (sales were approximately between 316 and 390). This implies that sales are influenced by transient factors such as offers and large order purchases, as opposed to gradual sales progression.

## Actions for improvement: clear out surplus stock for under-performing products, ensure that high performing sales products are available, implement a loyalty scheme within the UAE and analyse why there was a large sales increase in September so that this can be replicated.
